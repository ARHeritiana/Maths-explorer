\ExplorerTitle{High school · optimisation and olympiads}{Inequalities and equality cases}
\ExplorerSection{Before you begin}
Squares, factorisation and square roots.\par
\ExplorerSection{Ideas and method}
For every real \(u, u^{2}\geq 0\), with equality exactly when \(u=0\). Many inequalities reduce to this observation.\par
For \(a,b\geq 0, (\sqrt{a}-\sqrt{b})^{2}\geq 0\) gives \(a+b\geq 2\sqrt{ab}\), the arithmetic--geometric mean inequality. Equality holds exactly when \(a=b\).\par
Proving a bound is not enough to announce a maximum: an admissible equality case must attain it. Positivity and domain conditions are part of the argument.\par
\ExplorerSection{Worked example}
If \(a+b=10\) with \(a,b\geq 0\), then \((a-b)^{2}\geq 0\) gives \(100-4ab\geq 0\), so \(ab\leq 25\). The pair \(a=b=5\) attains 25, proving that it is the maximum.\par
\ExplorerSection{Explore}
Plot \(f(x)=x(10-x)\) for \(0\leq x\leq 10\) and move the point. Then prove the maximum without relying on the picture.\par
