On a $P(X  = 4) = \dfrac{2}{21}$ et $P(X = - 1) = \dfrac{19}{21}$.

On a donc $E(X) = 4 \times \dfrac{2}{21} + (- 1) \times \dfrac{19}{21} = \dfrac{8 - 19}{21} = - \dfrac{11}{21} \approx - 0,524$.
