## Mathematicians' timeline: binding revised editorial brief (31 October 2026)

This supersedes the **fixed 46 retained / 39 excluded** implementation instruction in the 30 October handoff and the earlier draft. No numerical cap or requirement that the 85 existing entries exhaust mathematical history is authorised.

1. **Genuinely major mathematicians:** include all major figures supported by serious historical/mathematical sources, including important figures missing from the existing timeline. Each must have a substantial, sourced **dedicated biography** describing their main original mathematical contributions and why those contributions matter, with **bidirectional links** between their timeline entry and the dedicated biography. An existing long biography should be reused or improved rather than duplicated. Short timeline summaries can remain for navigation.
2. **Other significant ("semi-major") mathematicians:** include those whose contributions and lives can be documented sufficiently for a meaningful short biographical account **inside the mathematicians' timeline**. A dedicated biography page is **not** required. Do not create filler or one-line names.
3. **Exclude thin, incidental entries:** do not keep a person solely because a theorem, puzzle, paper, historical anecdote or mathematical object on the site bears their name. Remove entries that cannot meet either category's historical substance threshold. **Do not move excluded people into another biography list just to preserve their names.** Existing discoveries remain in the *separate advances timeline* with accurate attribution to the discoverers, whether or not those people have biography entries.
4. **Reassess rather than mechanically keep/remove:** the 46 FR/EN expanded profiles and former 39-name exclusion list are **preliminary working material**, not a final whitelist or deletion order. Verify the 46, identify missing major mathematicians and intermediate figures, and reclassify any of the 39 if reliable evidence justifies them. Avoid quotas; classify by mathematical importance and the amount of trustworthy material available.
5. **Publication and acceptance:** factual claims, chronology, research contributions, original attribution and translations need independent historical review; changes must satisfy the project's two-agent publication gate and accessibility/browser checks. Distinguish the published people timeline from the advances timeline and from the dedicated `#biographies` page. **Documentation preparation is not evidence of a live update.**

**Suggested first independent timeline chunk:** inventory current `site/js/history-data.js` and existing dedicated biographies, propose a sourced major/intermediate/exclude classification (including omissions outside the original 85), and record uncertain cases without changing the served site. Subsequent chunks can verify and expand one bounded group before publishing.
