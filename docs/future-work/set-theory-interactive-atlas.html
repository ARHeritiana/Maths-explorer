Three ideas to remember

Elements are not subsets. The statements 2 ∈ A and {2} ⊆ A mean different things.

Order and repetition do not change a set. {1,2,2} = {2,1}.

Always specify the universe for complements. Aᶜ includes everything in U that is not in A.

For finite sets: |A ∪ B| = |A| + |B| − |A ∩ B|.
