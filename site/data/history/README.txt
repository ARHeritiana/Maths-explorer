History illustrations from Wikimedia Commons.

The single image register is site/js/history-images.js (served as js/history-images.js).
Every entry retains its original file URL, Commons file description, creator, credit,
licence name and URL, retrieval date, dimensions and image type.
Attribution is displayed on the associated history detail page and in reading view.
Images were resized to at most 480 x 600 pixels and converted to WebP; thumbnails
are cropped by CSS. No changes were made to the original source files.
CC BY-SA derivatives retain the original CC BY-SA licence linked in the register.
Public-domain and CC0 files retain their recorded status. Third-party images are
not covered by the repository content licence.
No invented ancient portraits or unverified image licences are included.
