Place value as grouping

A numeral records how a quantity was bundled: ten ones make a ten, ten tens make a hundred. Nothing in that rule is special to ten. Build numbers with counters, then change the base and watch the same rule hold.

Add counters to a column. When a column holds as many counters as the base, bundle them into one counter of the next column. Unbundle to go the other way. Bigger counters are worth more. In Blocks view, drag the 3D scene to look around.

Drag to orbit. Pinch or scroll to zoom. The focus buttons zoom in on one kind of block.

To write a number in another base, repeatedly make the biggest groups you can. Each remainder is the digit left loose in that column.

Digits above 9 use letters: A = 10, B = 11, up to F = 15. Tap ⚙ to show the base controls.
